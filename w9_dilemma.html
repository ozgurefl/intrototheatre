🎭 THE PLAYWRIGHT'S DILEMMA

Washington D.C. • 1953

House Un-American Activities Committee Hearing
📜 Your Situation
You are Eleanor Vance, a successful Broadway playwright. Your play "The Union Street" — a quiet drama about factory workers — has been running for six months.

This morning, you received a subpoena. The HUAC wants to question you about your "associations with subversive elements." Three actors and one stage manager who worked with you have been named. They want you to confirm their identities and name others.

If you cooperate, your next play will open as scheduled. If you refuse, you will be blacklisted — no theatre will hire you, your current play will close, and your reputation will be destroyed.

What do you do?
⚠️ Based on real testimonies of artists like Arthur Miller, Elia Kazan, and Dalton Trumbo.
